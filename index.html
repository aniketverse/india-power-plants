Scope: Electricity-generating stations from the historical WRI GPPD baseline plus the CEA/NPP daily station-wise (DGR-2) update feed. Industrial, captive, co-generation and private-sector stations are retained where they appear in the source records; no industry-name or sector filter is applied to the CEA feed. The CEA feed is limited to stations published in DGR-2 and is not a complete register of every captive generator. Its capacity field uses CEA monitored capacity (MW), which may differ from installed capacity. The WRI GPPD v1.3.0 baseline is historical and no longer maintained, so this is a combined reference inventory—not a certified current register. Cross-check current plant layers against the official ISRO–NITI Energy Map and CEA generator registry. Rooftop/distributed solar is excluded.

Data notes

  • WRI GPPD v1.3.0 is historical and no longer maintained.
  • Generation history in the source is mainly 2013–2019; it is not FY 2024–25 generation.
  • Missing coordinates prevent state assignment and map display.
  • Industrial, captive and co-generation records are included where present in the source data; they are not screened out.
  • Renewable projects are represented as individual records where present; the live VEDAS map also provides district-level renewable counts.
Official cross-check: ISRO–NITI Energy Map provides separate conventional and renewable plant layers; CEA's National Data Registry lists generating units by project, unit, fuel, state and status. These official sources are linked below for reconciliation. CEA DGR-2 additions are retained regardless of sector or industry; coverage is limited to stations present in the published report.

Sources

1. ListBase India Power Plants download page — 1,589 records advertised; data source WRI GPPD v1.3.0; CSV served from its CDN.
2. WRI Global Power Plant Database — fields include plant name, fuel, capacity, coordinates, owner, source and historical generation; WRI says the database is not currently maintained.
3. ISRO–NITI Aayog Geospatial Energy Map of India — conventional plant layers, renewable project layers and state-wise search.
4. CEA National Data Registry for Generating Units — project/unit/fuel/state/status register; includes captive and industrial generating units.
5. CEA Generation Reports — station-wise generation reports.
